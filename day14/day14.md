# Day 14 – AI Job Red Flag Detector

## Overview

For Day 14 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I explored how AI can help job seekers evaluate opportunities before investing time in applications and interviews.

Most candidates focus only on getting shortlisted, but an equally important skill is identifying whether a job opportunity is actually worth pursuing.

Using Claude's AI Red Flag Detector framework, I analyzed a real job description and company information to identify potential hiring risks, workplace concerns, and opportunity quality indicators.

---

# Objective

Analyze a Job Description (JD) and Company Information to identify:

- Unrealistic requirements
- Toxic workplace signals
- Remote work authenticity
- Hiring risks
- Company risks
- Positive signals
- Interview validation questions

# Key Red Flags Identified

## 1. Intern vs Full-Time Contradiction

### Severity: 9/10

The position title states:

> Web Developer Intern

However, the employment type is listed as:

> Full-Time

### Why This Matters

This can indicate:

- Internship-level compensation
- Full-time workload expectations
- No guaranteed conversion opportunity

A common hiring pattern where companies seek long-term contributions while offering intern-level benefits.

---

## 2. Work From Home Contradiction

### Severity: 8/10

The listing mentions:

- Work From Home benefits

But simultaneously states:

- In-Person work location

### Why This Matters

Possible explanations:

- Copy-paste job description
- Hybrid role not clearly defined
- Misleading job advertisement

Work mode should always be confirmed in writing.

---

## 3. Missing Salary Transparency

### Severity: 7/10

No clear compensation range was provided.

### Risks

- Salary negotiation difficulties
- Below-market compensation
- Lack of transparency

---

## 4. Ambiguous Growth Path

### Severity: 6/10

No information regarding:

- Conversion to full-time
- Career progression
- Performance evaluation process

# Smart Interview Questions Generated

### 1. Internship Clarification

Can you clarify whether this role is an internship or a full-time position?

---

### 2. Work Mode Clarification

The JD mentions both Work From Home and In-Person requirements. What is the actual working arrangement?

---

### 3. Conversion Opportunity

If this is an internship, what percentage of interns typically receive full-time offers?

---

### 4. Career Growth

What does the growth path look like during the first 12 months?

---

### 5. Compensation Structure

Could you explain the salary structure, benefits, and performance review process?

---

# Key Learnings

## Lesson 1

A job application is a two-way evaluation process.

Companies evaluate candidates, but candidates should also evaluate companies.

---

## Lesson 2

Contradictions inside job descriptions often reveal hidden risks.

Examples:

- Internship vs Full-Time
- Remote vs In-Person
- Flexible hours vs Mandatory office presence

---

## Lesson 3

Benefits like Provident Fund can indicate organizational maturity and legitimacy.

---

## Lesson 4

AI can significantly reduce the time required to evaluate job opportunities.

---

## Lesson 5

Asking smart interview questions helps uncover hidden issues before accepting an offer.
