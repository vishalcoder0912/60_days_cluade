## Key Learnings

1. Role prompts produce more specialized answers.
2. Different personas focus on different aspects.
3. AI becomes more useful when context is provided.
4. Founder persona emphasized business strategy.
5. Developer persona emphasized technical execution.

---

## Conclusion

Role-Based Prompting improves response quality by guiding AI to think from a specific professional perspective.
