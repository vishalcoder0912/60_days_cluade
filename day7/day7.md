# Recommended Effort Levels

## Low

Simple questions and quick answers.

## Standard

Daily coding and learning tasks.

## High

DSA, debugging, architecture discussions, project planning.

## Max

Research reports, career planning, startup strategy, and complex reasoning.

| Research            | Opus       | High        | Strong reasoning        |

---

# Biggest Mistakes To Avoid

1. Using Opus for simple tasks.
2. Using Low effort for complex problems.
3. Ignoring reasoning settings.
4. Always choosing the most powerful model.
5. Not matching model capability to task complexity.

---

# Biggest Learning

Choosing the correct model is as important as writing a good prompt.

Different models excel at different tasks, and reasoning effort can significantly impact output quality.
