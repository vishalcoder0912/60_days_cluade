# Day 2 - What Is Prompt Engineering

## Objective

Learn how Prompt Engineering improves AI output quality.

---

## What Is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing clear and structured instructions for AI systems to generate more accurate, relevant, and useful responses.

Instead of giving vague instructions, we provide context, goals, constraints, and expected output formats.
