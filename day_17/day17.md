# Day 17 – AI Vehicle Cost & Fuel Analysis Dashboard

## Challenge

Build an AI-powered Vehicle Cost & Fuel Analysis Dashboard using Claude by analyzing a CSV dataset and generating a complete interactive HTML dashboard.

---

## Objective

The goal of this project was to use AI as a Data Analyst and Dashboard Developer to:

* Analyze structured CSV vehicle data
* Calculate business metrics and KPIs
* Compare fuel types
* Evaluate vehicle operating costs
* Analyze environmental impact
* Create an interactive dashboard using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG visualizations

## Key Insights

### Insight 1

The fuel with the lowest operational cost per kilometer provided significantly better long-term savings than traditional fuel options.

### Insight 2

Environmental impact varied greatly between fuel types, with cleaner fuels showing substantially lower CO₂ emissions.

### Insight 3

Maintenance costs increased noticeably as vehicle age increased, highlighting the importance of ownership lifecycle planning.

### Insight 4

E85 fuel demonstrated an interesting trade-off between fuel price savings and fuel efficiency penalties.

### Insight 5

Data visualization made hidden trends much easier to identify compared to reading raw CSV data alone.
