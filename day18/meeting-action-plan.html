Quarterly Growth Strategy Meeting

Q2 Performance Review & Strategic Priorities

📋 Summary

Leadership reviewed Q2 performance against annual targets. Q2 revenue grew 12%, falling short of the 18% target due to enterprise deal slippage caused by procurement delays and additional security reviews. The meeting addressed conversion rate optimization, engineering capacity constraints, critical reporting performance issues affecting customer retention and sales, and budget approvals for website redesign and conference spending. Key decisions include deferring hiring plans, reprioritizing engineering work to address reporting performance, and avoiding public commitments on the August analytics dashboard release until engineering completes its milestone review.

⚡ Key Takeaways

Revenue Miss Q2 revenue 12% vs 18% target. 5 of 14 expected enterprise deals slipped to Q3 due to procurement delays and security reviews.
Reporting Performance is Critical Dashboard loading times and reporting performance issues are driving customer complaints, impacting retention (currently 94%), and affecting sales calls.
Engineering at Capacity Team is operating near capacity with analytics dashboard due August. Website redesign may require external contractors and cost impact unknown.
Lead Generation Improving Website traffic up 34% YoY, but conversion rates remain lower than expected. Website redesign and simplified demo process planned.
Multiple Approval Holds Customer conference budget pending vendor proposals. Hiring decisions postponed until Q3 forecast finalized. Analytics dashboard launch announcement deferred pending engineering milestone review.
